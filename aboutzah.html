Welcome to Pete Zah's Pizza, where passion meets the perfect pie!

This company was founded by our glorious creator, Pete Zah, on the belief that great pizza should be equal parts tradition and creativity, Pete Zah's Pizza has been serving up mouthwatering
slices to the community since day one. Whether you're a fan of the classics or looking to try something bold, we've got a pizza with your name on it (and maybe Pete's, too).

At Pete Zah's, everything is made with love—from our hand-tossed dough to our house-made sauces and fresh, locally sourced toppings using our traditional italian recipes.
Our team is all about bringing people together over a hot, cheesy slice in a laid-back, welcoming space.

So come hungry, leave happy, and remember: it's not just pizza. It's Pete Zah's Pizza.
